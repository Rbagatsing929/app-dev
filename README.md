
| # Stranger Things |
| * Stranger Things is a 1980s sci-fi horror series about the mysterious disappearance of a young boy in the town of Hawkins, Indiana. His friends, family, and the local police team up to investigate, uncovering a secret government lab, a parallel dimension called the Upside Down, and a telekinetic girl named Eleven who helps them battle monstrous creatures. The show blends supernatural horror with themes of friendship, conspiracy, and 1980s nostalgia.  * |
